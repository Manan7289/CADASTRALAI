What's actually novel here

  1. Rule-based compliance engine — every building footprint is checked automatically against the 30 m rail buffer, 15 m river buffer and adjoining government-land boundaries, with the legal basis cited alongside each alert.
  2. Temporal change detection — comparing two drone flights of the same ward surfaces new construction and footprint growth the moment it happens, not just a static snapshot. Toggle the flight date above.
  3. Confidence-ranked verification queue — every parcel carries a segmentation confidence score, so field staff know exactly which ones to check first, not all 13.
  4. One-click verification notes — findings are turned into a ready-to-route note for the revenue / railway / irrigation office instead of a manual write-up.
Parcels, buildings and violations shown here are simulated for demonstration. In production, footprints come from the U-Net / Mask R-CNN segmentation engine over real ORI + DSM−DTM height data, cross-checked against Bhu Naksha and SVAMITVA records — see our submission for the full pipeline.
