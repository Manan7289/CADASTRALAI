Process a drone survey image or video

Upload high-resolution UAV drone orthophotos, aerial survey stills (DJI, Autel, etc.), or drone flight videos. Our AI pipeline automatically extracts Physical Boundary Walls, Road Corridors, Building Plinths, and delineates Cadastral Land Parcels with 14-digit ULPIN and corner traverse points.

How the Drone Cadastral Engine Works

Unlike basic building detectors that rely on synthetic Voronoi heuristics, CadastraAI uses a true Cadastral Multi-Feature AI Pipeline:

  • Drone Video Keyframe Selection: Samples survey frames and uses Laplacian variance scoring to select the sharpest, motion-blur-free frame.
  • Physical Boundary Wall & Fence Extraction: Detects compound walls, fences, and demarcated boundary edges directly from drone imagery.
  • Planar Graph & Watershed Partitioning: Fuses road margins and boundary walls into closed cadastral land parcels, correctly classifying both built-up plots and vacant land.
  • SVAMITVA & ULPIN Standards: Assigns 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar identifiers, computes boundary traverse corner coordinates, and exports to AutoCAD DXF.
