Process a drone image or video

Upload a drone photo, satellite orthoimage, or a short video (its middle frame is used — this is not multi-frame stitching). Tell us roughly where it was taken and how wide an area it covers, and the same detection + parcel + rule-engine pipeline used for the Igatpuri demo runs on it.

What's different from the demo

The Igatpuri demo trains a RandomForest on real, verified OSM building labels for that specific colony. An arbitrary new upload has no labelled ground truth to train on, so building detection here falls back to an unsupervised brightness/texture threshold — it will find obvious rooftops but is less reliable than the trained demo model. Road, rail, water and government-land layers ARE still real: they're fetched live from OpenStreetMap for whatever location you give it.
